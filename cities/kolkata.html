WHY KOLKATA'S FIXED-ROUTE AUTOS OFFER UNPARALLELED FREQUENCY

Strict Fixed-Route Auto System

Unlike other cities where autos are hail-only, Kolkata autos operate on dedicated fixed feeder routes connecting metro stations to residential colonies. This creates unbeatable repeat frequency among regular commuters.

Massive Pedestrian & Market Exposure

Kolkata's bustling street life, traditional bazaars (Gariahat, Hatibagan), and vibrant cultural scene ensure that exterior vehicle ads are seen by millions of pedestrians daily.
