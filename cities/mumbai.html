WHY ADVERTISE IN MUMBAI WITH AUTOADS

Unmatched Traffic Density & Dwell Time

Mumbai's traffic patterns mean vehicles spend considerable time in slow-moving transit along Link Road, SV Road, Western Express Highway (WEH), and LBS Marg. This gives your exterior auto-rickshaw branding extended viewability with trailing motorists, passengers, and roadside pedestrians.

Suburban First-and-Last-Mile Transit

Auto-rickshaws serve as the primary lifeline connecting Mumbai suburban local railway stations and Metro lines (Lines 1, 2A, 7) to residential towers and corporate office parks in BKC, Mindspace Malad, NESCO Goregaon, and Powai.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS · MUMBAI ADVERTISING

❓ Where can auto-rickshaw ads operate in Mumbai?
Auto-rickshaws operate legally throughout Mumbai's Suburban District—from Bandra North to Dahisar in the West, and Sion North to Mulund and Thane in the East. They penetrate dense residential lanes and commercial bazaars where full-size buses cannot easily circulate.

If you are launching a retail store or clinic in Lokhandwala, Andheri, AutoAds clusters vehicle fleets operating primarily from local auto stands within a 3-5 km radius of your storefront to maximize repeat local impressions.
